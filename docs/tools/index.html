Kilobyte / Tools

Free browser-based image optimization tools.

Compress JPEG and PNG files, convert images to WebP and AVIF, resize exact pixel dimensions, or batch-process folders into a ZIP archive. Every utility runs locally on your device with zero uploads, no accounts, and no quotas.

Compress

Reduce image file size while keeping visual quality intact.

Compress Image

Optimize JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, or BMP locally with live before/after inspection.

Open Compress Image

Compress JPEG

Optimize JPG and JPEG photographs with live quality control and side-by-side detail check.

Open Compress JPEG

Compress PNG

Shrink PNG screenshots, logos, and transparent graphics via palette quantization or lossless OxiPNG.

Open Compress PNG

Convert

Convert JPEG and PNG images into smaller modern WebP and AVIF files.

JPG to WebP

Convert JPEG photographs into lightweight WebP copies with real measured savings.

Open JPG to WebP

PNG to WebP

Convert transparent PNGs and UI screenshots to WebP while preserving crisp alpha edges.

Open PNG to WebP

JPG to AVIF

Convert JPEG photos to compact AVIF files with native speed and WASM fallback.

Open JPG to AVIF

PNG to AVIF

Convert transparent PNG graphics and screenshots into ultra-compact AVIF files.

Open PNG to AVIF

Resize

Scale photos and graphics to exact pixel dimensions with aspect-ratio lock.

Resize Image

Resize photos to exact width/height or common web sizes with aspect-ratio lock and high-quality scaling.

Open Resize Image

Batch

Optimize dozens of images locally with bounded concurrency and ZIP export.

Bulk Image Compressor

Batch compress and convert dozens of photos in parallel with live totals and ZIP export.

Open Bulk Image Compressor

Running a WordPress website? Kilobyte automatically optimizes your images as you upload them, keeping every original Media Library file and link intact.
